# Bibliography

Status: draft, 24 November 2026.

## How to read an entry

Each entry gives:

- the author or issuing body;
- the year;
- the title and the edition;
- the publisher or journal;
- the link, ISBN or DOI;
- the verification status;
- what the source supports in these documents.

The verification statuses are defined in [README.md](README.md#conventions):

- **Verified (content):** the point cited was checked on the official text, the issuing body's page or its indexed
  text.
- **Verified (bibliographic):** only the bibliographic data were checked.
- **To be verified.**

A sentence that relies on a source verified only bibliographically, for more than its title, is marked «(content to be
verified)». Paywalled standards and books were not reproduced, and short quotations are attributed.
