## 1. Valuation norms and standards

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NRVT (Nederlands Register Vastgoed Taxateurs): register and regulations for valuers, including the regulation for residential property and its practice guidance. The guidance asks for reference transactions no older than two years where possible, and for a plausibility check of the report. | Confirmed | [NL-NRVT] |
| In September 2025 the ministry and NRVT signed an agreement to strengthen the supervision of valuations | Confirmed | [NL-NRVT] |

## 2. Area measurement

| Item | Status | Source |
