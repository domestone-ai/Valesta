## 2. Area measurement

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Superficie privative («loi Carrez»): the sale of a lot in a co-ownership must state it (art. 46, loi n° 65-557) | Confirmed | [FR-CARREZ] |
| Definition: floors of closed and covered rooms, after deducting walls, partitions, steps and stairwells; parts lower than 1.80 m excluded | Confirmed | [FR-DECRET-97-532] |
| Surface habitable: art. R156-1 of the CCH. It excludes, among others, unconverted attics, cellars, garages, terraces, loggias, balconies and verandas. | Confirmed | [FR-CCH-R156-1] |
