## Differences from the app pack

- **Common parts.** The spec dates the asbestos rule for common parts from 1 May 2025, while OVAM's pages mention the
  end of 2026 and 2027. A local professional should settle the date.
- **Regions.** Several rules are regional (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels). The pack should say which region each
  document belongs to.
