## 2. Area measurement

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DPR 138/1998, Allegato C sets the criteria of the *superficie catastale*: walls in full up to 50 cm, shared walls at 50% up to 25 cm, rooms lower than 1.50 m excluded, balconies and terraces weighted | Confirmed | [IT-DPR138-1998] |
| The pack calls it «superficie commerciale». DPR 138/1998 defines the cadastral area; the commercial area is a market convention. Whether the OMI price database refers to the Allegato C criteria is not confirmed here. | To be verified | — |

## Differences from the app pack

- **Area.** Explain the convention. Italian practice speaks of «superficie commerciale», often computed with the
  criteria of DPR 138/1998, Allegato C; whether the OMI database refers to them is still to be verified (see §2).
- **Reserved valuations.** Cite art. 120-duodecies TUB and the Banca d'Italia rules instead of «often UNI 11558
  certified».
- **Editions.** State the edition of the Codice and of the ABI guidelines.
