# Country cards

Status: draft, 24 November 2026. The 19 country packs of `src/js/taxonomy.js:126` (`const COUNTRIES`) are drafts
until a local professional reviews them, and the app says so.

## How each card was made

Each card covers the four topics of the pack:

1. valuation norms and standards;
2. area measurement;
3. typical documents;
4. who may sign valuations for mortgage lending.

For each item, the card says what the pack states and what was checked:

- **Sources.** Official legislative portals, the bodies that issue the standards and the regulators.
- **Confirmed.** The source was found and the point checked.
- **To be verified.** It was not, and the card says why.

The cards do not cover the other parts of the spec: obligations, anti-money laundering, data and maps, and the wording
of the agent's document.
