## 4. Valuations for mortgage lending: who signs

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| For Pfandbrief cover, the mortgage lending value rests on a valuation by an appraiser who is independent from the credit decision and has the required experience and knowledge (§ 16(1) PfandBG) | Confirmed | [DE-PFANDBG-16] |
| The BelWertV adds three requirements: a written report with an inspection of the property (§ 5); special knowledge, which is presumed for appraisers certified by a state or accredited body, for example under DIN EN ISO/IEC 17024 (§ 6); and independence from the loan, from brokerage and from the borrower (§ 7) | Confirmed | [DE-BELWERTV] |
| Other mortgage loans: CRR art. 229 applies (independent, qualified valuer) | Confirmed for the EU rule | [EU-CRR] |

## Differences from the app pack

- **Reserved valuations.** «Certified» is one way to show qualification under § 6 BelWertV, not the only one. Say
  «appraisers meeting §§ 6–7 BelWertV».
- **Energy law.** Update the name of the energy act if the portal confirms GModG as its new title.
