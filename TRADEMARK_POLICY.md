Version 2.0 — 24 November 2026

This policy tells you how you can use the names and the logos of Domestone S.r.l. with Valesta.

These are distinctive signs of Domestone S.r.l. (the "Marks"):

- the name "Domestone" and the Domestone logo;
- the name "Valesta" and the Valesta logo (the files in [brand/](brand)).

## 2. The licence and the Marks

The GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0) gives rights on the copyright and on the patents of Valesta. It
does not give rights on the Marks. As an additional term under section 7(e) of the AGPL-3.0, Domestone S.r.l. declines
to grant rights under trademark law for the Marks.

## 3. Uses that need no permission

You can use the Marks to refer to Valesta in a truthful way and in line with fair commercial practice (article 21 of
the Italian Industrial Property Code and equivalent laws). Examples:

- "This report was prepared with Valesta."
- "Based on Valesta" or "a fork of Valesta", for a modified version that has its own name.
- "Opens the files of Valesta", if this is true.

These uses must not suggest that Domestone S.r.l. made, approved or supports your product or your service.

## 4. Unmodified copies

You can distribute unmodified copies of Valesta, as published by Domestone S.r.l., with their name and logo.

## 5. Modified versions and forks

You can modify Valesta under the terms of the AGPL-3.0. A modified version that you distribute must:

1. have its own name, for example "Open Valuation". The name must not include or imitate "Valesta" or "Domestone";
2. replace the Valesta and Domestone logos with its own logos (the files in [brand/](brand));
3. not use a visual identity that people can confuse with the Marks.

## 6. No implied affiliation

Without written permission of Domestone S.r.l., you must not use the Marks in a way that suggests affiliation,
approval, partnership or official origin.

## 7. The logos

Except for unmodified copies (section 4), every use of the Domestone logo or of the Valesta logo needs written
permission of Domestone S.r.l.

## 8. Requests

Send requests for permission to Domestone S.r.l. through the contact details on www.domestone.com.
